Q3: What is the difference between Merging and Rebasing?

Answer: Merging combines two branches with a merge commit, connecting the history of the two branches and retaining a chronological log.
        Rebasing writes the feature branch's commit to the end of the target branch, making the history of the two branches a linear path for a clean commit log.
